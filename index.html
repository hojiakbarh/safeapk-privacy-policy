✓ Zero Data Collection & 100% On-Device Privacy

SafeAPK is built strictly on an offline-first, privacy-preserving security model. All heuristic file evaluations, APK structure dissections, permission vulnerability audits, and malware threat scorings are processed entirely on your physical device. We do not collect, monitor, store, sell, or transmit any user files or personal identifiable information (PII) to remote servers.

1. Overview & Purpose

This Privacy Policy provides clear, transparent information regarding how SafeAPK ("the Application", "we", "our") operates on Android devices. SafeAPK is a specialized mobile cybersecurity utility designed to protect users against malicious APK files, suspicious SMS grabbers, dropper anomalies, and rogue permissions before and after app installation.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure maximum privacy protection for our users, SafeAPK adheres to strict data minimization principles:

  • No User Account or Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email, phone number, or password.
  • No Financial or Banking Data: While SafeAPK offers an Emergency Financial Shield (allowing immediate one-tap hotline dialing and USSD shortcuts to freeze compromised cards during an attack), SafeAPK never accesses, reads, collects, or stores your credit card numbers, CVVs, passwords, or transaction histories.
  • No Location Tracking: The application does not request or track GPS, network, or background location.
  • No Remote File Uploads: When you select an APK file from storage for inspection, the binary and manifest headers are parsed in local volatile memory. The file is never transferred across the internet.

3. Device Permissions & Technical Justifications

SafeAPK requires specific system permissions solely to perform its essential cybersecurity functions. We never misuse or repurpose these permissions:

SafeAPK keeps external dependencies to the absolute minimum:

5. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

All scan history logs, security score records, and user preferences are saved locally on your device within the app's sandboxed private storage. You have complete control:

  • You can clear all scan logs at any time inside the app settings.
  • Uninstalling SafeAPK or clearing app data via Android Settings immediately and permanently deletes all locally cached scan logs and preferences.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K Compliance)

SafeAPK does not target or knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because no personal information is collected from any user, children's privacy is rigorously respected.
